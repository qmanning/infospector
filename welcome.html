Welcome to Infospector

You're looking at a page inside the stage. Load one of your own pages, click things, leave notes an assistant can act on. Here's the whole workflow.

  1. Load a page

    Press ⌘K (or click the search box), pick a page, or paste any URL from your site. Pages from your sitemap and links it has seen appear automatically.

  2. Pick a size

    The size button (top-left) lists phones, tablets and desktops; choose Custom to type one, or drag a corner of the stage. An orange ring means the stage is scaled to fit — the percentage says how much.

  3. Turn on Inspect

    Press ⇧⌘I or the Inspect button. Clicks on the page are swallowed; hover shows an element's size, name and selector; rulers appear (drag guides out of them). Hold Shift any time to peek — and Shift+click something to lock it in.

  4. Leave a note

    Click an element, then Add note. Notes live in draggable cards with a marker pinned to the element. Shift+drag selects an area; ⇧⌘+click builds a multi-selection.

  5. Hand it to an assistant

    Every note carries a unique selector, the component chain and box metrics. The notes button (top-right) copies them all as text; with the file bridge running they land in a JSON file an agent can read and mark as done.

Make it yours

Right-click the background outside the stage for patterns, colors and the glass look. Add ?setup to the URL to redo the first-run setup, ?doctor to check the install. The keyboard button under the stage — or ? — lists every shortcut.
